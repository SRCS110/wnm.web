Community Support

Community Partners.

Approved Community Partners are welcome to sell logo merchandise or run a fundraising raffle at the market. Over 36 years, the market has drawn more than a million visitors to downtown Santa Rosa — and community partners have played a real part in that, introducing neighbors to local organizations and opening doors to volunteering.
