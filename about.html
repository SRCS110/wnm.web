Our Story

37 years of bringing us together.

Since 1989, the Wednesday Night Market has been the heartbeat of Sonoma County summers. Born from a simple idea — bring people downtown, celebrate what's local — it's grown into the county's largest street fair and one of its most beloved traditions.

We're a non-profit run by local leaders and volunteers who give their time every week, all summer long. No outside investors. No corporate agenda. Just community people who love Santa Rosa and want to share it.

Our mission: build a strong community through an affordable, joyful market fair — free for every family in Sonoma County.
